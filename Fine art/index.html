About Us

Where Art Meets Excellence

Founded with a vision to nurture creative talent, New Age Institute of Fine Arts has been Delhi's most trusted fine arts coaching centre for over a decade. We specialize in comprehensive BFA entrance preparation while building strong foundational skills across all artistic disciplines.

Our institute provides a unique blend of traditional fine arts techniques and modern digital creativity, ensuring our students are prepared for top BFA programmes across India — including prestigious universities like Delhi University, BHU, Jamia, and MSU Baroda.
